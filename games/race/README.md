# ジャスティスRC / Justice RC

- Publish `games/race/` as its own static directory. The HTML reuses the already-published Justice Naomi costume/portrait UI. Gameplay, CSS, cover and the five approved MP3s are same-folder dependencies; reaction visuals reference previously released /games/submarine/portraits/ assets without duplicating them; no backend, install, or payment.
- Top-down two-lap RC race, five cars. Red car and yellow player marker. Pass lap one in 1st–3rd, or retire. Clear only if you cross the second finish in 1st.
- Arcade selectors: 初級｜オーバル, 中級｜S字, 上級｜ヘアピン. Controls: ←/→ or A/D; ↑ or W accelerator; ↓ or S brakes then reverses; R re-centers at current arc progress. Touch buttons support simultaneous steering and throttle. Changing course is available before or after a run. 100円 / もう100円！ are free arcade cabinet flourishes.
- Opponent contact or the player car's center reaching either painted curb spins the player in place for two simulation seconds; steering, pedals and reset cannot move it during the spin. Rivals continue moving while the player spins. There is 0.9s immunity from opponent collisions after release. A wall spin recovers just inside the boundary with an inward heading when needed; a sprite-only graze does not spin.
- Base rival speeds #1–#4 are 148, 138, 128, 118 track units/sec (same step of 10); per-course multipliers are .85, 1.0, and 1.06, respectively. Player maximum forward speed is 272. All valid laps require visiting the quarter track positions in order and crossing forward; reverse cannot add milestones and unrealistic between-sector jumps are rejected. Rivals win exact ties.
- The main view stays north-up, follows the red car and looks ahead; the fixed top-left minimap shows the full circuit, all five cars and the current camera rectangle.
- Reused voice reactions (not redubbed): `miss_uso` spin; `hit_yatta` a pass below first or first-lap qualification; `miss_oshii` denied on lap one; `endwon` nonwinning finish; `hit_justice` moving into first place or first-place finish. A move into first takes precedence over the ordinary passing line. Volume, SE and Voice independently controlled.
